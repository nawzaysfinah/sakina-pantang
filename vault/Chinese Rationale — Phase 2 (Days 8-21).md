## Principle
第二阶段从"止血护体"转为"补血养气"。急性期已过，现在身体需要主动重建——通过四物汤补血，通过膳食恢复气力，通过鱼胶和猪脚重建结缔组织。 The shift in this phase is from protection to active synthesis. The body's repair enzymes are now fully activated; feeding them the right substrates determines the quality of long-term recovery.

## Food
- Fish maw (花胶) is among the richest natural sources of Type I collagen peptides — the form most readily absorbed and directed to skin, joint cartilage, and uterine tissue repair.
- Pork kidney (猪腰) in sesame oil tonifies kidney Qi (肾气) — in TCM, the kidneys govern reproductive function, fluid metabolism, and bone marrow. Nutritionally, kidney is extremely high in CoQ10, iron, B12, and selenium.
- Snow fungus (银耳) contains beta-glucans that stimulate collagen synthesis via fibroblast activation — the same pathway targeted by expensive skincare treatments, delivered through food.
- Lotus root (莲藕) is rich in tannins (astringent compounds) that help close internal surfaces — its traditional role in "clearing" postnatal bleeding has a phytochemical basis.
- Papaya + fish tail soup (青木瓜鱼尾汤) is the classic galactagogue: green papaya contains papain and phytoestrogens that stimulate prolactin; fish tail provides omega-3 DHA essential for breastmilk composition.

## Herbs
- [[四物汤 (Sì Wù Tāng)|四物汤]] (Four Herb Decoction) is the most-studied formula in TCM pharmacology: [[Dang Gui (当归)|Dang Gui]] (当归) for blood, Chuan Xiong (川芎) for blood circulation, Shu Di (熟地) for blood nourishment, Bai Shao (白芍) for blood astringency. Together they address all four aspects of postnatal blood deficiency.
- Astragalus (黄芪) in this phase activates T-cell and NK-cell production — rebuilding immune function suppressed during pregnancy (the body deliberately lowers immunity to not reject the foetus).
- Burdock root (牛蒡) is a prebiotic inulin source that restores gut microbiome diversity — disrupted by birth and antibiotics — which directly affects breastmilk microbiome quality.

## Confinement
Weeks 2–3 correspond to the period when the Qi (气) that sustained pregnancy is at its lowest ebb. Chinese physicians describe this as the moment when "the river must be refilled before the wheels can turn again" — the logic behind the dense herbal and nutritional loading of this phase.
