Turmeric contains curcumin — a proven anti-inflammatory that reduces uterine swelling and supports liver detoxification of postnatal hormones. In [[Malay Pantang]] it's one of the two foundational warming spices (alongside [[Ginger (Halia Tua · 老姜)]]) used in jamu minum (drinks), tapel and param (herbal pastes), and everyday cooking through the 44 days.

Kunyit asam (turmeric + tamarind) is one of the most recurring jamu minum drinks across the whole confinement period — introduced early and returned to throughout.
