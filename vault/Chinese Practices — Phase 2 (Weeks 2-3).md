## Practices
- 💆 Light hair washing now permitted — use warm ginger-boiled water (姜水), dry hair immediately
- 🚶 Gentle movement within the home — short slow walks inside only, no exertion or lifting
- 🌬️ Continue avoiding cold and wind — the body is still open (毛孔未完全关闭)
- 🍵 Begin [[四物汤 (Sì Wù Tāng)|四物汤]] (sì wù tāng) — Four Herb Decoction: Dang Gui, Chuan Xiong, Shu Di, Bai Shao for blood
- 🛁 Herbal sitz baths: mugwort, ginger & lemongrass in warm water — wound healing & circulation
- 🤱 Papaya soup and fish maw soup for breastmilk — drink warm, eat consistently every day

## Herbal Drink
[[四物汤 (Sì Wù Tāng)|四物汤]] (sì wù tāng) — [[Dang Gui (当归)|Dang Gui]] (当归) + Chuan Xiong (川芎) + Shu Di Huang (熟地黄) + Bai Shao (白芍), simmered with pork rib or black chicken. The fundamental blood-building formula.

## Wisdom
"产后三分病，七分养" — After birth, 30% is illness, 70% is nourishment. Medicine heals the acute; food and rest heal the deep.
