"Wind entering the body" — a core concept in Malay postpartum medicine. The postpartum body is considered *terbuka* (open): pores, joints, and the womb are vulnerable, and the entire [[Malay Pantang]] system exists to protect this opening from cold, wind, and impurity while tissue heals and hormones rebalance.

Avoiding cold or raw food is believed to prevent masuk angin, which Malay medicine links to joint pain, bloating, and slow recovery that can persist for decades. Foods classified as *sejuk* (cooling) are believed to slow healing and chill the womb — the humoral-medicine root of many pantang food restrictions.
