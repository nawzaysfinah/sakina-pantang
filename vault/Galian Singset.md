Abdominal- and womb-tightening jamu, introduced in [[Malay Rationale — Phase 3 (Days 22-44)]]. Works with connective tissue that has had time to hydrate and soften — applying tightening botanicals too early is ineffective, since the tissue must first be restored.

This is one of the "stronger formulations" referenced in [[Jamu & Breastfeeding Jaundice Risk]] — mothers are generally advised to wait until baby's newborn jaundice has cleared before drinking galian singset at full strength.
