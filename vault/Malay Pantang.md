Malay & Indonesian postpartum confinement tradition, observed across the 44-day [[MOC - Postpartum Journey|postpartum journey]]. Built on the idea that the postpartum body is *terbuka* (open) and vulnerable — see [[Masuk Angin]] — and organised into three phases:

- [[Malay Rationale — Phase 1 (Days 1-7)]] — protection
- [[Malay Rationale — Phase 2 (Days 8-21)]] — active rebuilding
- [[Malay Rationale — Phase 3 (Days 22-44)]] — integration and return

Core recurring elements: [[Kunyit (Turmeric)]], [[Ginger (Halia Tua · 老姜)]], [[Urutan (Malay Massage)]], jamu minum (drinkable herbal tonics — see [[Jamu & Breastfeeding Jaundice Risk]] for safety), tapel/param (herbal pastes), pilis, and herbal baths.
