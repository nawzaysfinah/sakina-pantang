## Principle
最后阶段是"恢复活力"——气血渐充，百脉归常，身体开始真正属于自己。现在的重点是固本培元，为未来数十年打好基础。 This phase operates on a long-range logic: the investment made now determines recovery at 3 months, 6 months, and menopause. TCM practitioners say 坐月子坐得好 (a good confinement) is the difference between a woman who ages well and one who doesn't.

## Food
- 十全大补汤 (Ten Perfect Tonics) in this phase is prescribed because the body now has the digestive strength to absorb complex polypharmaceutical formulas — the same herbs given in Week 1 would be wasted on a gut too depleted to process them.
- Shaoxing rice wine (花雕酒) in cooking from Week 3+ serves multiple functions: it is a solvent that extracts fat-soluble herb compounds, a circulatory stimulant, and a natural preservative that extends the shelf life of confinement dishes.
- Bird's nest (燕窝) contains epidermal growth factor (EGF) — the same compound in expensive anti-ageing serums — along with sialic acid that supports infant brain development via breastmilk.
- Crab (蟹) is now permitted with ginger to neutralise its "cold" nature: the warming compounds in ginger (gingerols, shogaols) chemically counteract the vasoconstricting effects of crab's cooling proteins, making the combination genuinely safer than either alone.

## Herbs
- 十全大补汤 combines [[四物汤 (Sì Wù Tāng)|四物汤]] (blood) with 四君子汤 (Qi) plus Huang Qi and Rou Gui — addressing Qi, Blood, Yin, and Yang simultaneously. It is prescribed only in Phase 3 because building Qi before Blood is restored would cause excess heat (上火).
- [[Sesame Oil (麻油)|Sesame oil]] continues throughout all 44 days because linoleic acid (its primary fatty acid) is the structural precursor to prostaglandins — the signalling molecules that coordinate uterine healing, milk letdown, and wound closure.

## Confinement
满月酒 (Full Moon Celebration) on Day 44 is not merely cultural: communal celebration triggers a measurable oxytocin surge that reinforces maternal attachment, reduces postnatal depression risk, and — through the laughter and connection of community — activates the vagal brake, shifting the nervous system out of the stress state that birth necessarily induces. The party is medicine.
