The warming foundation of [[Chinese Zuo Yuezi]] cooking, used continuously across all 44 days — not just early on. Sesame oil warms the uterus, lubricates joints stressed by pregnancy, and contains sesamol, a potent antioxidant that crosses the blood-brain barrier, supporting postnatal cognitive recovery.

It continues throughout all 44 days because linoleic acid — its primary fatty acid — is the structural precursor to prostaglandins, the signalling molecules that coordinate uterine healing, milk letdown, and wound closure. See [[Chinese Rationale — Phase 3 (Days 22-44)]].
