Angelica root, a cornerstone Chinese postpartum herb. Contains ferulic acid and Z-ligustilide — clinically shown to relax uterine smooth muscle, improve blood flow, and exert oestrogen-like effects that ease postnatal hormonal transition.

The primary blood-building herb in [[四物汤 (Sì Wù Tāng)]], introduced from Week 2. Used in Dang Gui chicken soup from Day 1 onward as the foundational confinement soup for blood building.
