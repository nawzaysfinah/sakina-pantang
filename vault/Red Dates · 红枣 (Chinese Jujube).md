Chinese red dates (红枣, *hóng zǎo* — botanically jujube, not the Middle Eastern date fruit used in [[Malay Pantang]] as kurma) are high in saponins and polysaccharides that specifically stimulate red blood cell production — the biochemical basis for their traditional "blood-building" reputation.

Central to [[月子茶 (Red Date & Longan Tea)]], drunk from Day 1. Combined with longan and wolfberry, red dates form a synergistic iron–folate–antioxidant complex: iron from the dates, B12 co-factors from longan, and zeaxanthin/lutein from wolfberry for postpartum eye and neural recovery.

See also [[Red Date Tea & Breastfeeding]] for safety notes.
