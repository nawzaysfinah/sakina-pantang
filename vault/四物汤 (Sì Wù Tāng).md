Four Herb Decoction — the most-studied formula in TCM pharmacology, and the fundamental blood-building formula of [[Chinese Zuo Yuezi]]. Combines [[Dang Gui (当归)]] (blood), Chuan Xiong (blood circulation), Shu Di (blood nourishment), and Bai Shao (blood astringency) — together addressing all four aspects of postnatal blood deficiency.

Introduced in [[Chinese Rationale — Phase 2 (Days 8-21)]], simmered with pork rib or black chicken. Referenced again in [[Chinese Rationale — Phase 3 (Days 22-44)]]: the richer Phase 3 tonic (十全大补汤) is only introduced once Blood is restored by 四物汤 first — building Qi before Blood would cause excess heat (上火).
