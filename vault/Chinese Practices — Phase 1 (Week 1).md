## Practices
- 🚿 Bathe only with warm herbal water — boil mugwort (艾叶) or ginger; no cold water at all
- 🌬️ Avoid wind and cold at all times — keep windows closed, wear socks, cover head when outdoors
- 😴 Rest your eyes — minimal phone, no reading, avoid crying if possible (protect eyesight 保护眼睛)
- 🍵 Drink only warm herbal teas throughout the day — red date, longan, wolfberry; no plain cold water
- 🚫 No raw, cold, or "cooling" (凉性) foods — avoid watermelon, salads, cold drinks entirely
- 👶 Rest completely between feeds — the confinement nanny (月嫂, yuè sǎo) handles everything else

## Herbal Drink
[[月子茶 (Red Date & Longan Tea)|月子茶]] (yuèzi chá) — Simmer 6 red dates + 10 longan + 1 tbsp wolfberry + rock sugar in 600ml water for 20 min. Drink warm throughout the day in place of plain water.

## Safety Note
红枣桂圆茶 (hóng zǎo guì yuán chá) is one of the gentlest options for a breastfeeding mother and is generally considered safe to enjoy daily from Day 1 all the way through Day 44. The main things to watch are its natural sugar content — keep it moderate rather than heavily sweetened — and pairing it with plain water, since concentrated herbal teas alone can make postpartum constipation worse.

## Wisdom
"月子病，月子好" (yuèzi bìng, yuèzi hǎo) — Confinement illnesses are healed during confinement. What you invest in these 44 days pays dividends for decades.
