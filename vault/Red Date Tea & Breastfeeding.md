# Red Date Tea & Breastfeeding

Unlike concentrated [[Kunyit (Turmeric)|jamu]] (see [[Jamu & Breastfeeding Jaundice Risk]]), [[月子茶 (Red Date & Longan Tea)|red date tea]] doesn't carry the same early-postpartum caution. It serves mainly as a warm, comforting fluid intake — essential for milk supply and postpartum recovery — with a nutrient profile naturally rich in iron, potassium, vitamins A, C, and B-complex, and antioxidants from [[Red Dates · 红枣 (Chinese Jujube)|red dates]], longan, and wolfberry.

Two practical caveats, not safety risks: longans and red dates are naturally sweet, so keep the brew light-to-moderate without added refined sugar to avoid a blood sugar spike; and drinking heavily concentrated herbal tea without enough plain water alongside it can worsen postpartum constipation.
