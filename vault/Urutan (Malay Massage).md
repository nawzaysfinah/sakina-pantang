Traditional Malay postpartum massage, typically performed weekly starting Day 7. Urutan accelerates lymphatic drainage, breaks down adhesions from birth, and — through the oxytocin released by touch — directly supports breastfeeding.

The massage schedule intensifies in [[Malay Rationale — Phase 2 (Days 8-21)]] because myofascial tissue (fascia, ligaments, pelvic floor) becomes "settable" around Days 10–21 — practitioners describe the body as *lunak* (soft and receptive), the window to reshape and support healing structure. Frequency peaks then tapers in [[Malay Rationale — Phase 3 (Days 22-44)]], consolidating fascial alignment as structural recovery completes.
