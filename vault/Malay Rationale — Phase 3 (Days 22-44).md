## Principle
The final phase is about integration — not strict restriction, but conscious return. The body has done the deep work. Now the practices seal and solidify what was built, and begin preparing the mother to re-enter full life.

## Food
- Full flavour reintroduction (spice, variety, celebration foods) signals to the hormonal system that the crisis is over — cortisol drops, prolactin stabilises, and milk supply often increases as stress reduces.
- Celebratory meals like tumpeng and full Padang spreads serve a psychosocial function: communal eating re-activates social bonding hormones (oxytocin) and marks the end of the mother's vulnerability period.
- Continuing iron and protein-rich foods in this phase supports the ongoing synthesis of hair, skin, and connective tissue — the cosmetic recovery that mothers notice at 3–6 months postpartum is seeded now.

## Jamu
- [[Galian Singset|Galian singset]] (abdominal tightening herbs) in this phase works with connective tissue that has had time to hydrate and soften — applying tightening botanicals too early is ineffective; the tissue must first be restored.
- The final mandi bunga setaman (flower bath) is ritual and real: volatile oils from jasmine, rose, and ylang-ylang cross the skin-blood barrier and have documented anxiolytic (anti-anxiety) effects.
- [[Urutan (Malay Massage)|Urutan]] frequency peaks then tapers — the final massages consolidate fascial alignment and signal to the body's proprioceptive system that structural recovery is complete.

## Safety Note
By this phase, most babies have long cleared any newborn jaundice, so fuller-strength jamu — including [[Galian Singset|galian singset]] and the richer tonics — is generally fine once your pediatrician has given the all-clear. Keep listening to baby: any new fussiness, stool changes, or skin changes are still worth a quick check-in.

## Confinement
Day 44 is not arbitrary. It is the end of the "fourth trimester" — the developmental window when the infant's nervous system is most dependent on close maternal contact, and when the mother's body has completed its primary repair cycle. Many cultures independently converged on 40–44 days: the Quranic arba'een, the Chinese full month, the ancient Greek tessarakoste.
