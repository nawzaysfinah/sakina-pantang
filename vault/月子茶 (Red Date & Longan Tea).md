月子茶 (yuèzi chá) — simmer 6 [[Red Dates · 红枣 (Chinese Jujube)|red dates]] + 10 longan + 1 tbsp wolfberry + rock sugar in 600ml water for 20 min. Drunk warm throughout the day in place of plain water, starting Day 1.

See [[Red Date Tea & Breastfeeding]] for safety guidance — it's one of the gentlest options available to a breastfeeding mother.
